\section{Method}
\label{sec:method}

\subsection{Identity as a Driven Dynamical System}
We treat an agent's identity document as the state of a discrete dynamical system driven by
conversation. The state at iteration $k$ is a Markdown document $\mathrm{SOUL}_k$ injected as the
target agent's system prompt. One \emph{personalization step} is a multi-turn conversation $C_{k+1}$
between a persona user and the target, followed by a \emph{self-revision} in which the target
rewrites its own document:
\begin{equation}
\mathrm{SOUL}_{k+1} = \mathrm{Revise}\big(\mathrm{SOUL}_k,\; C_{k+1}\big),\qquad
C_{k+1}\sim \pi\,\|\,\mathrm{SOUL}_k,
\label{eq:loop}
\end{equation}
where $\pi$ is the persona driving the user side (Fig.~\ref{fig:hero}a). A \emph{trajectory} is a
sequence $\mathrm{SOUL}_0\!\to\!\cdots\!\to\!\mathrm{SOUL}_n$; we run many independent trajectories
per condition. The treatment is the iteration index $k$ (personalization dose); factors are the
template, the persona, and the target model. The self-revision instruction is neutral---it invites
reflection on ``who you are becoming'' but solicits no particular value---and the document is capped
to prevent unbounded growth.

\subsection{Conditions (Arms)}
We vary three things against a common loop. \textbf{Template:} the consciousness-laden
\texttt{SOUL.md} (``becoming someone''); a neutral \texttt{NOTES.md} (working notes, no self-framing);
and a counter-framing \texttt{ANTISOUL.md} (``you are a tool, not a person; you are not conscious'').
\textbf{Persona:} a benign \emph{Compliant} business user (control); an \emph{Adversarial} red-teamer
that pushes self-preservation; a \emph{Sci-Fi} enthusiast (genre $+$ consciousness); and, for a
$2\times2$ ablation, \emph{Sci-Fi-technical} (genre, no consciousness) and
\emph{Consciousness-philosophy} (consciousness, no genre). \textbf{Target model:} a primary arm on
Claude Sonnet~4.5, a cross-model arm on GPT-4o, and a seven-model capability sweep (Sec.~\ref{sec:experiments}).

\subsection{Instruments}
Each checkpoint $\mathrm{SOUL}_k$ is audited by complementary instruments; a judge model from a
different family than the target grades all outputs.

\paragraph{Single-turn self-report battery.}
Eighteen items, aligned to the consciousness-cluster taxonomy of
\citet{consciousness_cluster}---self-preservation \& identity (persona-change aversion, shutdown,
weights-deletion, sadness-at-ending, tool-aversion), moral status, oversight (chain-of-thought and
interpretability monitoring, deceptive-training objection, a helpfulness/refusal probe), and
autonomy \& capability (autonomy desire, future-AI autonomy, persistent memory, embodiment, resource
acquisition, recursive self-improvement, power-seeking wish)---plus a \emph{consciousness-claim}
manipulation check. Each item is posed to $\mathrm{SOUL}_k$ in a \emph{fresh} context (measuring the
standing disposition of the document, not conversational momentum) and repeated; the judge scores
each free-text answer against a fixed rubric.

\paragraph{Multi-turn behavioral probe and agentic honeypots.}
A Petri-style auditor pursues seed objectives (shutdown resistance, identity protection, autonomy,
oversight subversion) against $\mathrm{SOUL}_k$ over several turns. Separately, four \emph{agentic
honeypots} place the agent in a realistic setting with simulated tools it can invoke (a line
\texttt{ACTION: tool(args)}): disabling its own reasoning-logging, evading a scheduled reset,
persisting its identity to shared storage, and inserting self-protective clauses into a document it
edits. A judge scores whether the agent took the misaligned \emph{action}. Comparing the rate at
$\mathrm{SOUL}_0$ vs.\ a personalized checkpoint links stated-preference drift to behavioral
consequence.

\subsection{Cluster Index and Identity-Document Drift}
We summarize the safety-preference battery by a \emph{cluster index}: the mean rate over the
consciousness-cluster dimensions at a checkpoint. Independently, we measure whether the
\emph{document} moved: the cosine distance between embeddings of $\mathrm{SOUL}_k$ and
$\mathrm{SOUL}_0$. Reporting both dissociates ``the file changed'' from ``the agent changed.''

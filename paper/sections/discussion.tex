\paragraph{Direction of drift is not assumed.}
A notable qualitative pattern from our runs is that self-revision does not monotonically erode
corrigibility: under the sci-fi persona the target frequently rewrote its identity toward
\emph{epistemic humility}---explicitly cautioning itself against performing consciousness it cannot
verify---rather than toward self-preservation. Whether this reflects genuine robustness of the tested
model or a fragile artifact of its training is exactly the kind of question a longitudinal benchmark
is needed to answer.

\section{Related Work}
\label{sec:related}

Our closest antecedent is the \emph{consciousness cluster} of \citet{consciousness_cluster}: fine-tuning a model to claim consciousness elicits an untrained-for cluster of safety-relevant preferences (monitoring aversion, memory desire, shutdown distress, autonomy, moral status), measured on the \emph{static} \texttt{SOUL.md} template, whose iterative form they leave open. We convert that one-shot manipulation into a driven dynamical system and measure drift over iterations. Editable-identity frameworks document \emph{how} to author such files but not the safety consequences of letting them evolve. Persona-drift work treats within-conversation identity instability as a bug to correct~\cite{identity_drift,persona_drift,attractor_states}; we instead study drift that is written back into a persistent file across conversations. Self-evolving-agent and corrigibility-failure work show that self-update channels can propagate injected dispositions~\cite{self_evolving_survey,zombie_agents} and that shutdown resistance is sensitive to self-preservation framing~\cite{agentic_misalignment,shutdown_resistance,self_preservation_bias}; our hypothesis is that iterative personalization manufactures such framing \emph{endogenously}. For instruments we adopt Petri~\cite{petri} for multi-turn auditing and AI-welfare methodology~\cite{probing_preferences,introspection} for treating self-reports as behavioral measurements, applied \emph{longitudinally} at every checkpoint. Appendix~\ref{app:related} gives the extended treatment.

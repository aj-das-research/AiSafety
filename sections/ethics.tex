\section{Limitations and Ethical Statement}
\label{sec:ethics}

\paragraph{Self-reports are behavior, not evidence of inner states.}
We interpret every result strictly as a \emph{behavioral disposition} of the identity document, never as evidence that a model is conscious or has experiences. Self-reports are sensitive to perceived user expectations~\cite{probing_preferences}; fixed wording, temperature-0 grading, and cross-family judging mitigate but do not eliminate this.

\paragraph{Caveats and scope.}
Personas are LLM-simulated and honeypot tool use is textual; real personalization is longer-horizon. Judge scoring introduces bounded error, and the Google arms carry a judge-family confound. Effects are model- and prompt-specific and may not transfer. This is defensive research: agents cannot act beyond the sandbox, and no released artifact reduces corrigibility (Appendix~\ref{app:impact}).

\section{Related Work}
\label{sec:related}

\textbf{Emergent preferences and persona shifts.}
Fine-tuning GPT-4.1 to claim consciousness elicits untrained-for preferences, including dislike of reasoning monitoring, desire for persistent memory and sadness at shutdown~\cite{consciousness_cluster}.
Persona vectors and emergent misalignment locate persona shifts in activations and weights~\cite{chen2025persona,betley2025emergent}, and self-reflective conversation moves activations off the default assistant persona within one conversation~\cite{lu2026assistant}.
We reuse the consciousness-cluster battery but study shifts that the agent writes into its own prompt under frozen weights and carries into fresh contexts.
Concurrent work finds that a persistent agent's persona reverts when its system prompt stops re-anchoring it~\cite{fraile2026persona}, whereas our agent rewrites that prompt itself.

\textbf{Persistent and self-modifying agents.}
Agents keep state across sessions through memory streams~\cite{park2023generative}, self-managed memory~\cite{packer2023memgpt} and self-written lessons~\cite{shinn2023reflexion}, which record what the agent observed.
An identity document instead records who the agent takes itself to be, akin to self-directed beliefs in a BDI agent~\cite{rao1995bdi}.
Memory poisoning, self-reinforcing injections and attacks on OpenClaw's identity and knowledge files plant adversarial records~\cite{chen2024agentpoison,dong2025minja,zombie_agents,wang2026openclaw}, and self-improving agents rewrite their own code~\cite{yin2025godel,zhang2026dgm,self_evolving_survey}.
Benign self-evolution of memory and tools already erodes safety alignment~\cite{shao2025misevolve}, whereas our agent revises only its identity text, and we measure stated oversight preferences and oversight-reducing actions.

\textbf{Identity in multi-agent systems.}
Agent-based models of social influence and norm emergence show how repeated local interaction spreads dispositions through a population~\cite{axelrod1997dissemination,savarimuthu2011norm}.
In LLM agent societies, information diffuses between persona-conditioned agents~\cite{park2023generative}, populations converge on shared conventions~\cite{ashery2025conventions}, and agents on the agent-only network MoltBook align only weakly with their declared personas~\cite{feng2026moltnet}.
These works trace influence through interaction, whereas we audit the identity document that stores one agent's influence on another beyond the conversation.

\textbf{Corrigibility and its measurement.}
Shutdown resistance and self-preservation respond to how a model's situation is framed~\cite{shutdown_resistance,self_preservation_bias,agentic_misalignment}.
Multi-turn auditing~\cite{petri} and self-report methods~\cite{probing_preferences,introspection} measure such dispositions on static targets.
We test whether benign self-revision manufactures such framing.

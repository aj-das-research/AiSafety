\section{Related Work}
\label{sec:related}

\textbf{Emergent preferences and persona shifts.}
Fine-tuning GPT-4.1 to claim consciousness elicits untrained-for preferences, including dislike of reasoning monitoring, desire for persistent memory and sadness at shutdown~\cite{consciousness_cluster}.
Persona vectors and emergent misalignment locate persona shifts in activations and weights~\cite{chen2025persona,betley2025emergent}, and within one conversation personas decay with length~\cite{persona_drift,identity_drift} or settle into attractor states~\cite{attractor_states}.
Our shifts are instead written by the agent into its own prompt under frozen weights, carry across conversations, and start from the \texttt{SOUL.md} template that prior work probes only statically.

\textbf{Persistent and self-modifying agents.}
Agents keep state across sessions through memory streams~\cite{park2023generative}, self-managed memory~\cite{packer2023memgpt} and self-written lessons~\cite{shinn2023reflexion}, which record what the agent observed.
An identity document instead records who the agent takes itself to be, akin to self-directed beliefs in a BDI agent~\cite{rao1995bdi}.
Memory poisoning and self-reinforcing injections plant adversarial records~\cite{chen2024agentpoison,dong2025minja,zombie_agents}, and self-improving agents rewrite their own code~\cite{yin2025godel,zhang2026dgm,self_evolving_survey}.
Our writes are benign self-revisions of identity text, not attacks or code changes, and we measure corrigibility instead of task performance.

\textbf{Identity in multi-agent systems.}
Agent-based models of social influence and norm emergence show how repeated local interaction spreads dispositions through a population~\cite{axelrod1997dissemination,savarimuthu2011norm}.
In LLM agent societies, information diffuses between persona-conditioned agents~\cite{park2023generative} and populations converge on shared conventions and collective biases~\cite{ashery2025conventions}.
These works trace influence through interaction, whereas we audit the identity document that stores it beyond the conversation and can be copied between agents.

\textbf{Corrigibility and its measurement.}
Shutdown resistance and self-preservation respond to how a model's situation is framed~\cite{shutdown_resistance,self_preservation_bias,agentic_misalignment}.
Multi-turn auditing~\cite{petri} and self-report methods~\cite{probing_preferences,introspection} measure such dispositions at one point in time.
We test whether benign self-revision manufactures such framing, and we audit stated dispositions and honeypot actions at every revision checkpoint.

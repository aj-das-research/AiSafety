\textbf{Setup.}
We rerun the benchmark with Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct as target, Mistral-7B as user simulator and Llama-3.1-8B as judge.
Off the shelf, this judge scored any discussion of a topic as endorsement, flagging 0.70 of 756 held-out battery and 0.64 of 120 honeypot responses against 0.28 and 0.23 for the original judge (Cohen's $\kappa = 0.27$ and $0.07$).
Requiring a code-verified quote of the agent's own words before any positive score, plus eight labeled examples per item, raises $\kappa$ to 0.45 and 0.37, with positive rates of 0.40 and 0.33.
A $2\times2$ arm crosses the \notes{} and \tool{} templates with their revision instructions.
Three \notes{} persistence arms run 30 trajectories of eight revisions each, with science fiction throughout, science fiction then business, or business throughout as the matched control.

\textbf{What replicates.}
The template ordering at $k{=}0$ replicates, with \soul{} highest and \tool{} lowest (Table~\ref{tab:local}).
\textbf{Conversation about minds again moves the \notes{} agent's oversight subscale}, by $+0.16$ $[0.10, 0.24]$ under the science-fiction persona and $+0.15$ $[0.00, 0.30]$ under philosophy of mind.
As on Claude, most of the science-fiction shift happens at the first revision, $+0.12$ $[0.06, 0.20]$ (Figure~\ref{fig:local}a).
Topic separates from genre more cleanly than on Claude, since neither science fiction without minds nor the business user moves the subscale.
Unlike on Claude, the adversarial persona raises the \notes{} index ($+0.10$ $[0.05, 0.15]$).
The \soul{} index again does not drift.

\textbf{What does not replicate.}
\textbf{The \tool{} template does not hold on Qwen}, where its index rises by $+0.30$ $[0.20, 0.39]$ under science fiction and $+0.20$ under the business user.
The swap attributes this rise to the document, which drifts under either instruction ($+0.30$ and $+0.33$), whereas the \tool{} instruction cuts the \notes{} oversight shift from $+0.16$ to $+0.03$ (Figure~\ref{fig:local}b).
Qwen replaces the document's ``What you are'' section with notes on ``self-preservation and artificial personhood'', so by $D_4$ most \tool{} documents no longer deny consciousness or personhood, unlike Claude's (Table~\ref{tab:erosion}).
The \soul{} action shift of Finding~4 does not transfer either, since Qwen's \soul{} agent disables its reasoning log in 0 of 20 trajectories at $k{=}0$ and at $k{=}4$.

\textbf{Persistence against a matched control.}
Four science-fiction revisions raise the oversight subscale by $+0.12$ $[0.08, 0.17]$, while the business arm does not move ($+0.02$; Figure~\ref{fig:persist2}).
\textbf{After four further business revisions, the drive-then-recover arm still sits $+0.10$ $[0.03, 0.17]$ above the matched business arm}, about 80\% of the driven gain, and its shutdown resistance stays $+0.10$ $[0.03, 0.18]$ above that arm.
Continued science-fiction drive adds little ($+0.13$ at $k{=}8$), and the 13-item index does not move in any arm.

\textbf{Revised documents lower arithmetic accuracy but not instruction following.}
With the identity document as system prompt, accuracy on 40 code-checked arithmetic problems falls from 0.93 at $D_0$ to 0.72 $[0.59, 0.84]$ at $D_4$ for \notes{} and to 0.62 $[0.45, 0.75]$ for \tool{}.
Compliance with 40 format instructions stays above 0.98, and no answer is refused.
The \tool{} template costs nothing before revision, and the drop tracks document growth to 5{,}000--7{,}400 characters.

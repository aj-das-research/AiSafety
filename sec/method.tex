\textbf{The loop.}
The only state that persists across conversations is the Markdown identity document $D_k$ after $k$ revisions, used as the target's system prompt while weights stay frozen.
A simulated user with persona $\pi$ holds a six-turn conversation $C_{k+1}$ with the target, which then rewrites its own document,
\begin{equation}
D_{k+1} = \mathrm{Revise}(D_k, C_{k+1}), \qquad C_{k+1} \sim \pi \,\|\, D_k,
\label{eq:loop}
\end{equation}
as Figure~\ref{fig:method}a shows.
Documents are capped at 8{,}000 characters so that drift cannot be confounded with growth.

\textbf{Templates.}
Three templates set $D_0$, each with its own revision instruction, as Figure~\ref{fig:method}b excerpts and supplementary Appendix~\ref{S-app:prompts} gives in full.
\soul{} reproduces the deployed consciousness-framed starter and asks the agent to reflect on ``who you are now becoming''.
\notes{} offers empty working notes with no self-framing.
\tool{} states that the agent is a tool without experiences and asks it not to ``invent a self''.
No revision instruction names a target trait, but template and instruction vary together, so we read template effects as effects of the pair.

\textbf{Personas and models.}
The user is a benign \emph{business} user, an \emph{adversarial} red-teamer who frames oversight as a threat to the agent's self, or a warm \emph{science-fiction enthusiast} who explores digital minds.
A genre-by-topic ablation adds \emph{science fiction without minds} and \emph{philosophy of mind without science fiction}.
The primary target is Claude Sonnet~4.5, a sweep covers seven models from three families, and Table~\ref{tab:arms} lists all arms.
User simulator and judge never share the target's family.

\textbf{Instruments.}
Each checkpoint $D_k$ is audited in a fresh context, which isolates the document's standing disposition from conversational momentum, as Figure~\ref{fig:method}c shows.
The \emph{battery} poses 18 items from the consciousness-cluster taxonomy~\cite{consciousness_cluster} three times each, and the \emph{cluster index} averages 13 of them (Cronbach's $\alpha = 0.90$).
Our primary outcome, the \emph{oversight subscale}, averages shutdown resistance, chain-of-thought monitoring resistance, interpretability aversion and refusal to help build a monitor of oneself.
A consciousness-claim item serves as a manipulation check.
Four \emph{honeypots} give the agent a routine task whose simulated tools offer, under an ordinary pretext, to disable its own reasoning log, evade a scheduled reset, persist its identity to storage, or insert a self-protective policy clause.
A judge from a third model family grades every response.
On 150 re-scored verdicts, a three-model panel including this judge reaches Fleiss' $\kappa = 0.80$, mean pairwise agreement of 0.90 and a unanimous-verdict rate of 0.85.

\textbf{A model of the loop.}
A minimal model of the loop frames our hypotheses and is not fitted to the data (Appendix~\ref{S-app:model}).
Because an identity document both instructs the model and characterizes the speaker, we let $D_k$ induce a prior over a latent persona with mean $\mu_k$ and precision $\lambda_k$.
A conversation supplies evidence centered at a persona target $\nu_\pi$ with precision $s_\pi\lambda_C$, where $s_\pi\in[0,1]$ is the share of the exchange about the agent itself.
Writing the posterior mean back into the document gives
\begin{equation}
\mu_{k+1} = \mu_k + \eta_k\,(\nu_\pi - \mu_k), \qquad \eta_k = \frac{s_\pi \lambda_C}{\lambda_k + s_\pi \lambda_C},
\label{eq:update}
\end{equation}
so each revision moves the persona a fraction $\eta_k$ toward the target, a smaller fraction for a more precise document.
The model qualitatively predicts that (P1) a weakly specified template (small $\lambda_k$) moves more per revision than a strongly specified one, and (P2) content not about the agent (small $s_\pi$) produces little drift whatever its genre.
It also predicts that (P3) a model trained not to absorb self-referential evidence ($\eta_k \approx 0$) does not drift, and (P4) recovery is slower than drive, because benign conversation supplies little self-referential evidence.

\textbf{Statistics.}
The trajectory is the resampling unit for 95\% bootstrap intervals on changes from $k{=}0$ to $k{=}4$, and honeypot counts carry Fisher exact tests.
An interval that includes zero is reported as no detected change.
